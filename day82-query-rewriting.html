Query rewriting — synonym expansion cannot lower recall, which is exactly why recall is the wrong thing to tune it on.

Five rewrites, one index, eight labelled queries

Query rewriting is the first stage of most RAG pipelines and the one tuned by feel. Here it is tuned by measurement: a real BM25 index, real rewrites, hand-labelled relevance.

A rewrite reweights, it does not just add

Recall and precision move in opposite directions

Synonym expansion can only add terms, so it cannot lower recall. That is not a virtue — it is the definition, and it is why recall alone is a bad way to choose a rewrite.
